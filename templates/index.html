02 · LICENSING

Start with the licence that matches your activity

The Mining Commission lists several mineral rights and trading licences. For small-scale mining, the key mining right is the Primary Mining Licence (PML).

⛏

Primary Mining Licence (PML)

For small-scale mining. The Commission describes PMLs as Division C licences for small-scale operations and states a maximum initial capital investment threshold of US$5 million.

Official licence information ↗
🏪

Broker Licence

Allows a licensed broker to buy minerals from buying stations specified in the licence and sell to a licensed dealer. A broker licence does not authorize export.

Check current rules ↗
🌍

Dealer Licence

A dealer licence supports mineral trading and authorizes export, subject to the applicable requirements and permits. Licence validity and eligibility should be confirmed with the Commission.

Check current rules ↗
1

Identify your activity

Mining, processing, buying, brokering or exporting may require different permissions.

2

Confirm the area and mineral

Check the proposed area, mineral type and whether another mineral right already covers the land.

3

Prepare the application

Use the Mining Commission's current application procedure and forms. Do not rely on an old checklist copied from social media.

4

Operate within the licence

Respect licence boundaries, safety, environmental requirements, reporting duties and applicable fees.

Important: Licence requirements and fees can change. Always verify the current procedure directly with the Mining Commission before paying an agent or starting an operation.
03 · BUYING, SELLING & EXPORT

Keep the gold trade inside the legal system

Before you sell

  • Confirm who is legally entitled to buy your mineral.
  • Use an authorized mineral market or buying centre where applicable.
  • Ask for valuation/transaction documentation and keep your records.
  • Confirm how purity, weight, royalty and inspection/clearance charges affect the transaction.
  • Do not transport or sell gold through an informal route because someone promises a higher price.

Export essentials

The Mining Commission states that mineral right holders and dealer-licence holders can export, while broker-licence holders are not authorized to export.

04 · PROCESSING PLANTS

How to choose a leaching or elution plant

Plant selection should start with legal authorization, ore characteristics, recovery objectives, worker safety and environmental controls — not simply the cheapest machine.

01

Know your ore

Use representative sampling and appropriate testing to understand grade, mineralogy and how the ore responds to processing.

02

Check the licence

Confirm that the plant and its operator hold the permissions required for the activity and location.

03

Check containment

Look for controlled process areas, tailings management, drainage and measures that prevent contaminated water or material leaving the site.

04

Check worker safety

Require appropriate PPE, ventilation, emergency arrangements, training and documented operating procedures.

05

Verify recovery

Compare independently measured recovery and operating costs. Ask for records rather than relying on a vendor's headline percentage.

Vat leaching

The Mining Commission has identified vat leaching as a common processing approach in small-scale mining. When assessing a facility, focus on licensing, containment, drainage, tailings control, worker protection and verified recovery.

Elution

Elution facilities recover gold from loaded carbon. For a small miner, verify plant authorization, secure carbon handling, ventilation, emissions control, emergency systems, operator competence and production records.

⚠
Safety first

This website intentionally does not provide chemical recipes, dosing instructions or step-by-step hazardous-process instructions. Use licensed specialists and the Mining Commission's current safety and environmental requirements.
